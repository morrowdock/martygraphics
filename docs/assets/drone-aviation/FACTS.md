* **Engine** says what the MartyGraphics engine did with the claim: `solved` (the engine computed it), `checked`
  (a `check` in the document passed), `counted` (a `repeat` count), or `not checked` (the engine shows the words

  gives it. `model` means the sentence is about the drawn model, not about the world. Each row ends with the
  fact-check result: PASS in both checks, or FIX with the check that asked for it and what changed.
