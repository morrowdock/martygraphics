Slider-crank: rotation to back-and-forth

[00:00:00.000] 1. A slider-crank turns spinning motion into back-and-forth motion, as in an engine or a pump.

[00:00:07.866] 2. Input: the crank turns. Its pin travels in a circle of radius 18 mm.

[00:00:13.600] 3. The connecting rod (62 mm) links the crank pin to the piston and pushes and pulls it.

[00:00:20.466] 4. The piston slides along a straight line. Its stroke is twice the crank radius: 36 mm per turn.
